What we do

  • FDM 3D printing of custom parts, jigs, fixtures and prototypes
  • 3D modelling and CAD drawing from a part, photos or measurements
  • Scale models for architecture, projects, products and education
  • Replacement plastic parts that are broken or no longer available
  • Contract 3D printing (fason) for companies, including small batches
  • Parts and personalised assistive aids for wheelchairs and disabled users. Parts that directly affect safety (brakes, wheels, load-bearing frame, electrics) may not be suitable for 3D printing, so we assess the function first; suitability cannot be guaranteed for every part.

How it works

1) Send your file, or photos and measurements, with quantity and intended use by WhatsApp or phone. 2) We check suitability and send a quote. 3) After you approve, we produce the part. 4) Collect it at our workshop in Osmangazi, Bursa, or receive it by cargo within Türkiye.

Customers outside Türkiye

We do not ship printed parts abroad. For customers outside Türkiye we offer remote 3D modelling and design: send photos, drawings and measurements by WhatsApp, and we prepare the 3D model and deliver it online as a digital file. Contact us to discuss your project.

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about ordering from Bursa, Türkiye.

How do I get a quote for 3D printing?

Send your STL, 3MF or STEP file, or photos and measurements of the part, together with the quantity and what it will be used for, by WhatsApp or phone. We check whether the part is suitable and reply with a quote.

Which materials do you print with?

We print with filament (FDM) printers. Options such as PLA, ABS, ASA and TPU are considered depending on the part and on availability; the material is confirmed when we quote.

Do you ship parts?

Yes. Parts can be collected at our workshop in Osmangazi, Bursa, or sent by cargo within Türkiye. Cargo cost and time depend on the part and the address and are given in the quote. We do not ship printed parts outside Türkiye. If you are abroad, we offer 3D modelling and design only, delivered online as digital files.

How much does 3D printing cost?

There is no fixed price list. The price depends on the size and volume of the part, the material, print time, quantity and whether 3D modelling is needed. We quote after seeing your file or measurements.
